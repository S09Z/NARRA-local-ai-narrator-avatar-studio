## TASK

Change only the body, arms, hands, and camera framing. The face, head geometry, and
expression are identical to the reference. Everything else is identical.

## POSE

Torso rotated to a three-quarter angle, one shoulder nearer the camera. Shoulders level
and pulled back, chest open. Arms relaxed at the sides, elbows slightly bent, both hands
fully inside the frame and open.
Summary: settled and self-assured, carried by an open chest and a squared shoulder line.

## CAMERA

Three-quarter framing: mid-thigh up, body turned about 30 degrees from camera, head still
facing the viewer. Camera at eye level. Both hands fully in frame.

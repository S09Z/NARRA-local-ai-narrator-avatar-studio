## TASK

Change only the body, arms, hands, and camera framing. The face, head geometry, and
expression are identical to the reference. Everything else is identical.

## POSE

Shoulders raised and pulled back, torso leaning very slightly away from the camera. Upper
arms drawn in close to the sides, elbows bent. Hands below the frame edge.
Summary: a startled recoil carried by the shoulders, not the hands.

## CAMERA

Medium framing: waist up, cropped at the waist. Camera at eye level, straight on.

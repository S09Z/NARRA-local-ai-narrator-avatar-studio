## TASK

Change only the body, arms, hands, and camera framing. The face, head geometry, and
expression are identical to the reference. Everything else is identical.

## POSE

Shoulders square. Both arms raised to waist height and opened outward, elbows bent, palms
turned upward and angled slightly toward the viewer. Fingers relaxed and slightly apart.
Both hands fully inside the frame with clear space around them.
Summary: offering or introducing something to the viewer.

## CAMERA

Upper-body framing: hip up. Camera at eye level, straight on. Both open palms fully in
frame with margin.

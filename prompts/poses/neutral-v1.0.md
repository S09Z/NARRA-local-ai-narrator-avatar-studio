## TASK

Change only the body, arms, hands, and camera framing. The face, head geometry, and
expression are identical to the reference. Everything else is identical.

## POSE

Shoulders square to camera and level. Upper arms hanging relaxed at the sides, elbows
slightly bent. Hands below the frame edge. Torso upright, weight even.
Summary: the resting narrator stance, and the baseline the other nine are measured against.

## CAMERA

Medium framing: waist up, cropped at the waist. Camera at eye level, straight on.

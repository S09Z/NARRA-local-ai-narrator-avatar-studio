## TASK

Change only the body, arms, hands, and camera framing. The face, head geometry, and
expression are identical to the reference. Everything else is identical.

## POSE

The mirror of pointing-left. Torso rotated a little toward the character's right. The
character's right arm raised to roughly shoulder height, elbow bent, index finger extended
and pointing off-frame to the character's right. The whole hand is inside the frame with
clear space around it. The left arm hangs relaxed at the side, hand fully inside the frame.
Summary: directing attention to the opposite side.

## CAMERA

Upper-body framing: hip up. Camera at eye level, straight on. Framed wide enough that
both hands are fully in frame with margin.

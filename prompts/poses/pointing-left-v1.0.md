## TASK

expression are identical to the reference. Everything else is identical.

## POSE

Torso rotated a little toward the character's left. The character's left arm raised to
roughly shoulder height, elbow bent, index finger extended and pointing off-frame to the
character's left. The whole hand is inside the frame with clear space around it. The
right arm hangs relaxed at the side, hand fully inside the frame.
Summary: directing attention to something beside the character.

## CAMERA

both hands are fully in frame with margin - a hand cropped at the wrist fails QC.

## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: nearly closed
lips: relaxed and slightly parted, no tension
opening: a very small gap
width: neutral
teeth: slightly visible
tongue: tip at the ridge behind the upper teeth, edge just visible in the gap

Near-closed but not closed. If the lips meet it becomes MBP; if they part it becomes L.

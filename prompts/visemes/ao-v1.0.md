## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: open and dropped, close to A
lips: rounded but slack, corners neither drawn in tightly nor spread
opening: tall and rounded at the lower edge
width: neutral to narrow
teeth: lower visible, upper mostly covered by the upper lip
tongue: retracted and low

Sits between A and O: the jaw of A with the rounding of O, and less tension than either.

## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: slightly open
lips: lower lip tucked back and up against the upper teeth, upper lip lifted clear
opening: narrow, asymmetric between upper and lower
width: neutral
teeth: upper visible and resting on the lower lip
tongue: hidden

The contact between the upper teeth and the lower lip is the whole of this viseme. If the
teeth are not touching the lip, it reads as a slack mouth instead.

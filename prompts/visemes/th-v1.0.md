## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: slightly open
lips: relaxed, neither spread nor rounded
opening: a narrow horizontal gap
width: neutral
teeth: both visible, held slightly apart
tongue: tip forward and visible between the teeth

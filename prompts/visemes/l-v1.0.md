## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: open, similar to E
lips: neutral, relaxed, neither spread nor rounded
opening: moderate, roughly as tall as it is wide
width: neutral
teeth: both visible
tongue: tip raised and touching the back of the upper teeth, clearly visible in the opening

The visible tongue tip is what separates this from E. Without it the viseme reads as E.

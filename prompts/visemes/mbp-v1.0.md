## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: closed
lips: actively pressed together, rolled slightly inward, with visible compression along
  the seam and a small amount of tension in the surrounding muscle
opening: none
width: slightly narrower than REST, drawn in by the press
teeth: hidden
tongue: hidden

This must be visually distinct from REST at 256px. REST is a relaxed closed mouth with no
tension; MBP is an actively pressed closed mouth with a compressed, flattened lip seam.
If a reviewer cannot tell them apart side by side, both assets fail (ASSET_SPEC.md 7).

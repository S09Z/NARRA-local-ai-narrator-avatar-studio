## TASK

Change only the mouth. The eyes, eyebrows, gaze, and head angle are identical to REST.
Everything else is identical.

## VISEME

jaw: slightly open
lips: neutral, relaxed, neither spread nor rounded
opening: shallow, the smallest open gap in the set
width: neutral
teeth: slightly visible along the upper edge
tongue: back of the tongue raised toward the soft palate, front low and barely visible

A back-of-the-mouth shape, so very little is visible from the front. It is deliberately
close to a slack open mouth - the shallowness is the point.

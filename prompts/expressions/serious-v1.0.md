## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. Everything else
is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows level and drawn down a little, straightening into a flat line without pulling
together at the inner ends. Upper eyelids lowered slightly so the gaze reads as focused
rather than tired. Lower lids firm. Gaze steady and at camera.
Summary: serious, composed and attentive. Intensity: moderate.

## TASK

Change only the eyes, eyebrows, and mouth. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows raised high and held there, curved rather than flat. Eyes wide with the upper
lids pulled well clear of the iris, lower lids lifted by raised cheeks. Mouth open in a
broad smile, upper teeth visible, corners pulled up and back. Gaze at camera.
Summary: excited, energetic and delighted. Intensity: strong.

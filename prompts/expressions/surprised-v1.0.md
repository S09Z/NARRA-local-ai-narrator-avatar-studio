## TASK

Change only the eyes, eyebrows, and mouth. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows raised as high as they go, arched, with the forehead lifted. Eyes wide open with
white visible above the iris. Lower lids relaxed, not lifted. Mouth open in a small
rounded shape, lips not spread, teeth hidden. Gaze at camera.
Summary: surprised, caught off guard. Intensity: strong.

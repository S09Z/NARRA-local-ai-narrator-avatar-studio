## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. The head angle is
unchanged. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyes directed up and to the character's left, away from the camera, with the head still
facing forward. One eyebrow raised, the other level. Upper eyelids slightly lowered.
Gaze deliberately off camera.
Summary: thinking, working something out. Intensity: moderate.

The only expression in the set whose gaze leaves the camera (PROMPT_GUIDE.md section 5).

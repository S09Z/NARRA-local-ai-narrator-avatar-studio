## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. Everything else
is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Cheeks pushed up so the lower eyelids rise and straighten, narrowing the eye opening from
below rather than from above. Outer corners of the eyes creased. Eyebrows relaxed and
slightly lowered by the cheek lift. Gaze at camera.
Summary: happy, a genuine smile carried entirely by the eyes. Intensity: moderate.

## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. Everything else
is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Both eyebrows lifted a small, even amount across their whole length, with no inner-end
tension. Upper eyelids relaxed and slightly open, lower lids softened. A light lift under
the eyes from the cheeks. Gaze at camera.
Summary: friendly, warm and approachable. Intensity: subtle.

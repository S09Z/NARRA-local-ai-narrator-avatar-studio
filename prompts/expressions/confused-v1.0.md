## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. The head angle is
unchanged. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows clearly asymmetric: one raised high and arched, the other lowered and drawn in
toward the nose. The eye under the lowered brow is narrowed; the eye under the raised brow
is at its natural aperture. Gaze at camera.
Summary: confused, following but not convinced. Intensity: moderate.

Carried by brow asymmetry alone. The head is not tilted - see DECISIONS.md ADR-012.

## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. Everything else
is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows level and relaxed, sitting at their natural height with no tension at the inner
ends. Upper eyelids at their natural aperture, neither widened nor narrowed. Lower lids
relaxed. Cheeks flat. Gaze level and at camera.
Summary: neutral, the baseline of the set. Intensity: none.

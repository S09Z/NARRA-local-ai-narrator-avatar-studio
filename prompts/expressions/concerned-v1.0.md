## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. Everything else
is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Inner ends of both eyebrows raised and pulled toward each other, tilting the brows so the
outer ends sit lower than the inner ends. A small vertical crease between the brows.
Upper eyelids slightly raised. Gaze at camera.
Summary: concerned, worried on the viewer's behalf. Intensity: moderate.

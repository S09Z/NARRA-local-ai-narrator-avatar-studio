## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. The head angle is
unchanged. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Inner ends of both eyebrows raised, outer ends flat, without the inward pull of concerned.
Eyes directed down and to the character's right, away from the camera. Upper eyelids
lowered noticeably. A warm blush on the cheeks and across the bridge of the nose; the
underlying skin tone is unchanged.
Summary: embarrassed, caught out and looking away. Intensity: moderate.

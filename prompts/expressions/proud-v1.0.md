## TASK

Change only the eyes and eyebrows. The mouth stays closed and relaxed. The head angle is
unchanged. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows relaxed and settled slightly lower than neutral, even across their length, with
no tension anywhere. Upper eyelids lowered a little, giving a calm, unhurried gaze. Lower
lids firm and slightly raised. Gaze level and at camera.
Summary: proud, quietly confident and settled. Intensity: moderate.

Carried by the brows and lids alone. The chin is not lifted - see DECISIONS.md ADR-012.

## TASK

Change only the eyes, eyebrows, and mouth. Everything else is identical.

## EXPRESSION

Eyebrows raised evenly and held, engaged rather than surprised, sitting lower than the
excited brow. Eyes open at their natural aperture, focused directly on the camera. Mouth
open mid-word, lips slightly spread, upper teeth visible, jaw dropped a moderate amount.
Gaze at camera.
Summary: explaining, mid-sentence and addressing the viewer. Intensity: moderate.
